# Search evaluation sets

Used to measure how well tällmi finds thoughts (`scripts/eval-search.sh`). None of this is user data, and none of it ships with the app.

## Synthetic journal (`synthetic-journal/`)

A year of an invented person's voice journal: about 1,000 thoughts (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026), German with English mixed in, voice-memo style, lengths from "Yeah." to long rambles.

- **`catalogue.json`** is the world the thoughts are written from:
  - the narrator, Jonas
  - 39 topics: people, places, work, health, home, money, feelings, hobbies, events, dreams
  - a storyline in eight parts
  - topics that must **never** appear
- **`parts/part-N.jsonl`** holds the thoughts, written by language-model agents from the catalogue, each tagged with the topics it touches.
- **`retag/retag-N.jsonl`** is a second, uncapped tagging pass that replaces the first one: the first pass was limited to four topics per thought and had a few wrong tags.
- **`queries.jsonl`** is generated by `make_queries.py`: 182 searches in seven kinds.
  - `exact`: words that appear in the text
  - `part`: part of a compound or a short form
  - `paraphrase`: German, other words
  - `english`
  - `question`
  - `combo`: two topics, both must be present
  - `none`: a never-mentioned topic, so the right answer is *nothing*
- **`build.py`** merges everything into `corpus.jsonl` and `queries.jsonl`.
  - The right answers to a search are the thoughts tagged with all of its topics.
  - Person tags are also completed from names in the text, including misspellings.

Because the right answers come from tags, scores are comparable between variants, not absolute truth. A thought that mentions a topic in passing may be found and still counted as wrong.

## Controls (downloaded by `scripts/download-eval-data.sh`)

| Set | Language | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `germanquad` | German | `mteb/germanquad-retrieval` (Wikipedia passages and questions) | CC BY 4.0 |
| `squad` | English | `rajpurkar/squad` v1.1 validation (Wikipedia paragraphs and questions) | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Files are pinned by revision and SHA-256, and converted into the same format. They are not checked in.

## Format

- `corpus.jsonl`: `{"id", "text", "date"?}`
- `queries.jsonl`: `{"id", "text", "kind", "relevant": [corpus ids]}`
