the idea

äh is the sound of thinking.*

talk the way you think, with pauses and restarts, in your language. the äh stays in. messy is good, and it works.

*in german.

kept

every word stays.

your thought stays exactly as you said it, äh included, next to everything you've said before.

connected

and finds what belongs together.

people, places, topics and feelings link up across your thoughts, in your language. worked out on your mac, never sent anywhere.

speak.

press ⌃⌥T anywhere on your mac. tälli appears next to your cursor and listens. say what's on your mind, press again, and it's kept.

transcribed on your mac, in your language.

write.

some thoughts want more care. write those instead, in the same place, with headings and lists when you want them.

spoken and typed thoughts sit side by side, and you search them together.

your words stay exactly as you said or typed them. tällmi never rewrites them.

privacy

your thoughts
are yours.

not big tech's. not an ad network's. not ours either. a journal only works if you can say anything in it, so tällmi keeps everything on your mac, and has no way to send it anywhere.

no internet, by design.

the app is built without network access. transcription, search and answers run on your mac's own chip, in flight mode too.

no account. no cloud.

nothing to sign up for, and no server that holds your thoughts. there is no tällmi server.

sealed, even when synced.

every thought is encrypted with a key in your mac's keychain. sync through any folder you like: what lands there is unreadable. your recovery key is the only spare.

plain files, yours to take.

export everything as markdown and audio whenever you want, readable in any text editor, with or without tällmi.
