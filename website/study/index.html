the story

six beats, short, top to bottom. two pillars carry it: the äh (say it the way it comes, write when you want to craft it) and privacy (your thoughts are yours). every beat ends one scroll away from the call.

  1. think out loud.the tagline, tälli alive beside it, and the call right there: try it · contribute.
  2. the ähthoughts don't arrive finished. no need for the right words first.
  3. speak or writetalk to get it out. write when a thought wants care. one small demo piece shows it.
  4. your thoughts are yourson your mac only, no account, sealed, plain files. proven on the page itself.
  5. try it · help build itthe call, in full: what you need to run it, and the ways to contribute.
  6. closethink out loud, once more. tälli peeks.
